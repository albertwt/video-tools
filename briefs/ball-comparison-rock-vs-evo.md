## FILES I'M SENDING
1. Main clip: B-roll showing both balls (grip close-ups, me handling/bouncing them). Its original audio is bad, so MUTE it completely

## CONCEPT
First-person verdict on two basketballs I actually play with. Not a spec review. It's "the ball I grab for pickup, and why." Audience is casual pickup players; the goal is "that's literally me," not "good to know."

## FORMAT
- Vertical 1080x1920, 20–30 sec (match the voiceover length)
- Voiceover drives the edit: cut the visuals to what I'm saying
- Auto-captions on the voiceover: bold, centered, kept clear of the balls
- Optional low background beat, ducked well under my voice

## STRUCTURE (sync to the voiceover; my wording may differ slightly from this)

## EDIT
- Scrub the main clip and pick the best moment for each beat; no single shot longer than ~6 sec
- Hook text on screen 0–3s: "the rock vs evo", large, top third
- Small on-screen labels ("THE ROCK" / "EVO") when each ball is the focus
- If the main clip runs short, reuse the photo with a different crop/zoom rather than repeating the same footage
- Hold the final frame ~1 sec

## DON'T
- No bounce-test framing (the verdict is about feel, not a measurable test)
- No prices, specs, or "best ball under $X"
- No zooms or effects that cover the grip close-ups

## How it actually went (notes for next time)
- **Voiceover length:** the rough script in the table was only ~40 words (~12s). Plan ~80–100 words for 20–25s.
  The raw recording was 42s, mostly pauses; tightening pauses to ~0.2s brought it to 26s with every word kept.
- **Price:** the recording mentioned "$50 or 60" despite the DON'T list. The phrase was cut cleanly in the edit.
- **No "both balls in one shot" footage existed,** so the Gap beat used the photo. Film one if you want it.
- **The clip was mirrored** (front camera). Fixed with a horizontal flip; check brand text reads correctly.
- **Music:** none added. Use the platform's built-in music library when posting.
- **Getting big files in:** a 60 MB clip can't come through the Drive connector. Either attach it in chat or
  set the Drive file to "Anyone with the link" so it can be downloaded directly.
